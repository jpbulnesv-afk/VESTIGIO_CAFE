Origin

Two ranges, one standard of care

Every lot we offer comes from farms we know personally — no anonymous cooperative blends. Altitude, variety, and processing are chosen deliberately, then verified before a single bag leaves Honduras.

Cordillera de Montecillos

Santiago de Puringla & upper La Paz

1,600–2,000 masl. Home to our Geisha and Pacamara lots — small plots on steep, cloud-covered slopes where cherry ripens slowly and unevenly, which is exactly why the cup is so distinct.

Montaña de Comayagua

Los Hornos & Sanpedrana communities

Around 1,600 masl. A tight-knit growing community producing our Sidra and Parainema lots, with a strong culture of experimental processing passed between neighboring farms.

NFT Certification · Non-Fungible Tokens

Every lot, verified forever

Before a lot ships, both sides agree on a cupping profile through an SCA-based arbitration. That agreement — score, notes, and lot data — is what gets certified, so neither party can quietly revise it later.

Certification runs on Cardano, built on the eUTXO model — the same lightweight, verifiable accounting logic Bitcoin pioneered. We chose it as common ground the two ecosystems already share, with room to connect further as that architecture matures.

Payment can move as a stablecoin, a native token, or Bitcoin — whatever settles cleanest for your treasury.

Where this is headed

Royalties

Certificate holders could earn a share when a lot's provenance is resold or licensed downstream.

Buyer discounts

Holding a certificate from a past harvest could unlock preferred pricing on the next one.

Direct auctions

Future rare lots could be offered first — or only — to holders, before anyone else sees them.

These mechanics are still being designed — we're building them alongside our first buyers, not before them.
